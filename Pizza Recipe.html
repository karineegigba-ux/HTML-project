If you are pizza lover do not sleep on this amazing recipe. This is the most delicious, mouth- watering pizza you will ever try!

Let's get started!

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup of warm water
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 packet of active dry yeast
  • 1 clove of garlic (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon of black pepper
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • Your favorite pizza toppings (e.g., tomato sauce, cheese, pepperoni, vegetables or maybe pineapple)

Base Making Instructions

Instructions (please follow them carefully):

  1. In a large bowl, pour all dry ingredients like flour, sugar, salt and yeast.
  2. With a dry wooden spoon mix all dry ingredients till they are evenly mixed.
  3. Add lukewarm water gradually while mixing.
  4. When it becomes firm knead it to form a smooth and elastic dough.
  5. Leave to rise in a warm place for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.

Sauce Making Instructions

You are almost there, Welldone!

  1. In a dry hot pan, add a teablespoon of olive oil and let is sit for 3 minutes.
  2. add garlic, salt, and pepper, then simmer with crushed tomatoes for 15-20 minutes.
  3. Let the sauce cool before using it on your pizza.
  4. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).

Assembly Instructions

Now it's time to assemble your pizza!

  1. On a flat surface with flour sprinkled over it, roll out the dough to your desired thickness.
  2. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  3. Put the dough into the preheated oven and leave for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
  4. Add your favorite toppings and bake for an additional 5-10 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

Now all the hardwork has paid off!

Serve with chilled drink or juice of choice and a dip of any sauce of choice.

Bon Appetit!
